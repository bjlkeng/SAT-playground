## DEEP DIVE: is the gap capability or trajectory? → CAPABILITY (inprocessing)

Instrumented every loss cell: kissat `-v` (full solve, mechanism ID — kissat
is deterministic so mechanism/conflict-count is contention-immune) vs solver12
`SAT_STATS_JSON` (300 s window: elimination %, conflict rate, which
inprocessing fired). Raw: `log/gap-read-2026-07-21/deepdive/` (`*.kissat.out`,
`*.s12.out`, `COMPARISON.txt`).

**Verdict: the losses are NOT trajectory rerolls. They are a real,
mechanism-level capability gap in INPROCESSING depth.** kissat wins by
*shrinking the formula* (elimination + kitten SAT-sweeping + equivalence
substitution) so its search runs on a much smaller problem; solver12 searches
the un-shrunk formula and cannot close it even with more conflicts.

### The three dominant capability gaps (ranked by evidence)

1. **Kitten-based SAT sweeping is effectively INERT in solver12.** kissat's
   kitten sub-solver runs 90 k – 18 M solves per instance; solver12's sweep
   finds 0 – 826 equivalences/backbones. This is the single biggest gap and it
   touches every loss cell. `lockchart_g1` is the cleanest demonstration:
   kissat solves it in **396 k conflicts** on the back of **10.9 M kitten
   solves**; solver12 has 0 elimination and 0 sweep and grinds raw. Even when
   solver12's sweep *does* fire (oski15a01: 826 finds), kissat's is ~450×
   more productive (379 k).

2. **Variable-elimination depth.** On gate/circuit cells kissat reaches
   72–88 % elimination vs solver12's 43–56 %. kissat's gate-aware BVE +
   equivalence substitution (`substituted`: 10 k–3.5 M vars; pj2008 = 3.52 M!)
   collapses the circuit; solver12's congruence finds few merges on the
   miters (Bubble/booth = 0) because its congruence is weaker/root-biased.

3. **Inprocessing SCHEDULING.** kissat interleaves substitute (60–111
   rounds), tiered vivify (tier1/2/3/irredundant to round ~40), congruence,
   sweep and walk *continuously* throughout search. solver12 gates
   inprocessing on a **1 M-conflict cadence** + a sweep-guard, so on the
   slow-conflict giants it fires **zero** inprocessing: goldcrest (474 conf/s
   → ~0.85 M in 1800 s < 1 M) and lockchart (330 conf/s → ~0.59 M < 1 M)
   **never reach the first inprocessing round in a full run**. This is a
   concrete, fixable defect — the cadence should be tick/time-budgeted, not
   conflict-counted.

Secondary: tiered vivification (kissat tier1/2/3/irredundant vs solver12
single LBD≤6 + delay cursor); failed-literal probing + HBR (`SAT_PROBE`/
`SAT_HBR` are `ParkingLot` — not implemented; kissat probes on every cell).

### The decisive "not-trajectory" proof
- **TT_C406**: solver12 does **1.13 M conflicts and FAILS**; kissat solves in
  **170 k conflicts** (6.6× fewer) because it eliminated 67 % of vars and
  kitten-solved 824 k. More search, worse result → capability, not luck.
- When the winner solves by *shrinking* the formula and the loser searches the
  *un-shrunk* one, a seed reroll cannot close it. Confirmed on 10/12 cells.

### The genuinely trajectory/search-bound minority (2 cells)
- **fixedbw** (149 vars, preprocessing-immune): pure resolution-hard UNSAT.
  kissat refutes at 12.1 M conflicts; solver12 flails past ~50 M. A *search-
  heuristic* gap (branch/restart/phase quality), not inprocessing — and still
  not a reroll (solver12 is not "close").
- **rbsat1375** (documented flipper): both land near ~6 M conflicts; kissat's
  sweep (85 k) tips it to SAT. This one is genuinely marginal/trajectory.

### solver12's counterpart capability (the other direction)
solver12 *wins* on XOR/parity structure kissat cannot touch: `xor_op` ×2
(`SAT_GAUSS` + `SAT_PAIR_ABS_REFUTE`, 1–2 s vs kissat >600 s), plus oddball,
Kakuro, MVRoundRobin, case1. So the two solvers have *different* capability
frontiers — solver12 owns algebraic/parity reasoning; kissat owns
circuit-collapse inprocessing (sweep + deep elimination).
